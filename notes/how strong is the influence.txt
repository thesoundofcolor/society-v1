Just a useless reference for doing output edge summaries for 'Anatomoy of the 42'

10% = Negligible: Barely detectable; almost non-existent.
20% = Minimal: Present in small amounts; minimal impact.
30% = Slight: Recognizable, but minor (akin to "light" or "annoyance" level).
40% = Sparse: Scattered or thin; noticeable but thin distribution.
50% = Moderate: Mid-range; clearly present and typical.
60% = Substantial: A solid, significant, or ample amount.
70% = Heavy: High in concentration or volume; weighty.
80% = Intensive: Concentrated, rapid, or profound action.
90% = Extreme/Severe: Highest intensity, approaching maximum; harsh.
100% = Total/Absolute: Complete and total; no exceptions
