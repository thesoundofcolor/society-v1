
--------------------------------------------------------------- Active ------------------------------------------------------------------------|

Addiction - prevalence of dependency on substances or behaviors that override self-control and disrupt normal functioning.

Agriculture - capacity to produce food and sustain agricultural output for a population.

Authoritarianism - degree to which power is centralized and individual freedoms are restricted by authority.

Birthrate - rate at which new individuals are born into the population over time.

Corruption - extent to which power is abused for personal gain within institutions or systems.

Cost of Living - overall expense required to maintain a basic standard of living within a society.

Crime - prevalence of unlawful behavior and violations of societal rules.

Deathrate - rate at which individuals die within a population over time.

Discrimination - degree to which individuals or groups are treated unfairly based on identity or characteristics.

Diversity - variety and representation of different cultural, ethnic, and demographic groups within a society.

Edu: Access - availability and ability for individuals to obtain education.

Edu: Cost - financial burden required to receive education.

Edu: Quality - effectiveness and standard of educational systems in producing knowledge and skills.

Employment - availability and stability of jobs and workforce participation.

Energy Production - capacity to generate and supply energy to support societal needs.

Equality - degree to which individuals have equal rights, opportunities, and outcomes.

Family Orientation - degree to which a society prioritizes family structure, reproduction, and generational continuity over individual autonomy and personal choice.

Freedom of Speech - extent to which individuals can express ideas and opinions without censorship or punishment.

Gun Rights - extent to which individuals are allowed to own and use firearms.

HC Access - availability and ability for individuals to obtain healthcare services.

HC Cost - financial burden required to receive healthcare.

HC Quality - effectiveness and standard of healthcare systems in treating and preventing illness.

Homelessness - prevalence of individuals lacking stable and permanent housing.

Immigration - flow of individuals entering a society from external regions.

Infrastructure - quality and availability of physical systems such as roads, utilities, and public works.

Innovation - capacity for developing new ideas, technologies, and solutions.

Law Enforcement - presence and activity of systems that enforce laws and maintain order.

Liberty - degree of individual freedom and autonomy in personal decision-making.

Market Freedom - extent to which voluntary exchange and economic activity operate without restriction.

Mental Health - overall psychological well-being and stability of individuals within a population.

Military - capacity for organized defense, including funding, personnel, and operational capability.

Minimum Wage - legally mandated lowest compensation for labor.

Pollution - level of environmental contamination affecting air, water, and land.

Poverty - prevalence of individuals lacking sufficient resources to meet basic needs.

Public Trust - degree of confidence individuals have in institutions and societal systems.

Rule of Law - consistency, predictability, and impartial application of laws, independent of status or influence.

Sickness/Disease - prevalence of physical illness and health conditions within a population.

Spirituality/God - degree of religious or spiritual belief and influence within society.

Taxes - level of financial contribution required from individuals and entities to fund government operations.

Tech - level of technological development and integration within society.

Transportation - availability and efficiency of systems used to move people and goods.

Welfare Support - extent of assistance provided to individuals in need through social programs.

Institutional Trust – degree of public confidence in government, institutions, and societal systems.

Resilience – ability of a society to absorb shocks and recover from disruptions without systemic collapse.

Climate Stability – consistency and predictability of climate patterns affecting environmental and societal systems.

CO2 Emissions – total greenhouse gas output contributing to atmospheric carbon concentration and climate change.

Regulation – breadth and restrictiveness of formal rules, requirements, and legal constraints across society.

Surveillance – extent of monitoring by state or institutions over individual behavior and activity.

Migration – net movement of people across regions, including both internal displacement and cross-border flows.

Border Security – strength and enforcement of controls regulating entry and exit across national boundaries.

Judicial Severity – harshness of sentencing, penalties, and consequences imposed by the legal system after enforcement.

Inflation Rate – rate at which the general price level of goods and services increases over time.

Purpose/Meaning - extent to which individuals feel a sense of direction, value, or meaning in life.

Wage Gap – disparity in earnings between demographic groups, typically measured across gender or identity lines.

Stigma – level of negative social judgment or discrimination toward specific behaviors, identities, or conditions.

Patriarchy – degree to which social, political, and economic power is disproportionately held by men.

LGBTQIA+ Inclusion – degree of social, legal, and cultural acceptance and protection of LGBTQIA+ individuals.

Sexual Freedom – extent to which individuals can express sexuality without legal restriction or social repression.

Creativity – capacity for novel idea generation, artistic expression, and innovative thinking within a population.

Sex Ratio (M⇆F) – distribution balance between male and female populations within a society.
