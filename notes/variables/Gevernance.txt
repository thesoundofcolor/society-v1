1. Judicial Severity
What it controls: Harshness of sentencing, punishment scale, retribution vs leniency
Core tension: deterrence vs over-penalization / system abuse
Key idea: Severity doesn’t equal justice, it’s just force applied after the fact

2. Rule of Law
What it controls: Consistency, fairness, predictability, equality under law
Core tension: stability vs rigidity
Key idea: This is an “integrity layer”

3. Regulation
What it controls: Volume and complexity of rules governing behavior (especially economic/social)
Core tension: protection vs friction
Key idea: Too little → chaos/exploitation, too much → stagnation/circumvention

4. Law Enforcement
What it controls: Presence, capacity, and activity of policing bodies
Core tension: safety vs overreach
Key idea: Enforcement is the execution arm but not the law itself
