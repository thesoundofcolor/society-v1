# Possible Variables

##In order of importance

Rule of Law - consistency, predictability, and impartial application of laws, independent of status or influence.

Institutional Trust - degree of public confidence in government, institutions, and societal systems.

Resilience - ability of a society to absorb shocks and recover from disruptions without systemic collapse.

Climate Stability - consistency and predictability of climate patterns affecting environmental and societal systems.

CO2 Emissions - total greenhouse gas output contributing to atmospheric carbon concentration and climate change.

Regulation - breadth and restrictiveness of formal rules, requirements, and legal constraints across society.

Surveillance - extent of monitoring by state or institutions over individual behavior and activity.

Migration - net movement of people across regions, including both internal displacement and cross-border flows.

Border Security - strength and enforcement of controls regulating entry and exit across national boundaries.

Judicial Severity - harshness of sentencing, penalties, and consequences imposed by the legal system after enforcement.

Inflation Rate - rate at which the general price level of goods and services increases over time.

Wage Gap - disparity in earnings between demographic groups, typically measured across gender or identity lines.

Stigma - level of negative social judgment or discrimination toward specific behaviors, identities, or conditions.

Patriarchy - degree to which social, political, and economic power is disproportionately held by men.

LGBTQIA+ Inclusion - degree of social, legal, and cultural acceptance and protection of LGBTQIA+ individuals.

Sexual Freedom - extent to which individuals can express sexuality without legal restriction or social repression.

Creativity - capacity for novel idea generation, artistic expression, and innovative thinking within a population.

Sex Ratio (M⇆F) - distribution balance between male and female populations within a society.
