Notes for this influence:

Agriculture → Pollution = +0.31

Strong direct positive influence. Agriculture is a major source of pollution for water, air, and soil systems. Pollution is directly caused by fertilizer runoff, livestock waste, pesticides, and various other chemical byproducts. 

Nutrient runoff is a large source of eutrophication, which is the excessive enrichment of water bodies (with nutrients like phosphorus and nitrogen). While emissions from manure and fertilizer greatly contribute to air pollution. 

The relationship is clearly direct and substantial, especially under intensive agricultural systems.

## Shaping Notes:

1) Monotonic positive
- Higher-order controlled
- Polynomial / blended monotonic shaping
- Steady rise at low-to-mid levels
- Stronger acceleration through intensive and industrialized agriculture
- Mild upper taper where mitigation and regulation begin to constrain additional pollution

2) Linear
- Consistent rise

~Evan Levy 4/20/26
