Notes for this influence:

Agriculture -> Spirituality/God = +0.25

[Range: +0.18 and +0.28]
 
Strong increase; reliance on land fertility, weather, and seasonal cycles evidently drives the formation of spiritual systems centered around divine control of natural forces.

Agricultural societies consistently develop structured religious practices: harvest rituals, fertility rites, traditional responsibilities, and priestly mediation. 

These patterns are foundational to many modern religions, including those in Western societies, where agricultural origins shaped their structure and agrarian symbolism.

In modern industrialized systems, agriculture no longer directly drives daily spiritual behavior, but its historical influence remains embedded in religious institutions and traditions.

Shaping Notes:

Monotonic increasing
Early-to-mid acceleration (subsistence dependence phase)
Soft saturation at high levels (industrial buffering reduces marginal impact)
