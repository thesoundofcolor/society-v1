Notes for this influence:

Agriculture → Transportation = +0.29

This is a strong direct positive influence. Agriculture is one of the main catalysts behind transportation networks. Moving  large volumes of goods, often perishable, from production regions to storage, processing, and markets abroad creates immediate pressure for transport expansion. Over time, that expansion slows as the constraint shifts from building more capacity to optimizing what already exists.
